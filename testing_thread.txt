Game Information
Title: Teeworlds
URL: https://github.com/teeworlds/teeworlds

Instructions:
Ready to run. No additional setup required. Launching opens a menu (D-Pad + A/B) to choose the online client, a local/LAN dedicated server, or a local server with AI bots (nheir's bMod) for solo play.

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|Left Stick| Move |
|D-Pad| Spectator prev/next; on-screen keyboard nav when open |
|A| Jump |
|B| Hook |
|X| Fire |
|Y| Scoreboard |
|L1| Previous weapon |
|R1| Next weapon |
|L2| Hold for second layer (team chat/whisper/chat log/statboard/vote/screenshot/emoticon/spectate) |
|R2| Fire (trigger) |
|Select + D-Pad Down| Toggle on-screen keyboard (OmniOSK) |
|Guide| Menu |
